Good to Know

❄️

Snow Decides

Northeast Ohio snow is unpredictable. Ski dates may move or be cancelled, and we will look for a replacement activity when the snow does not show up.

📧

Email Confirmations

We confirm or cancel by email, generally at least two hours before the scheduled start. See our weather policy for details.

🎿

Skis for the Season

Rented skis are handed out at the October kickoff and returned at the April hike. See membership for rental details.

Come to What Fits

Nothing on the calendar is required. Members are welcome at everything, and the Peek’n Peak day in January is explicitly optional.
