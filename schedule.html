2026-2027 Season Schedule

Roughly one event a month through the winter, every other month the rest of the year

Most events are Sunday afternoons. Winter sessions depend on snow, so we schedule more than we expect to hold and cancel when conditions do not cooperate. Exact dates are posted on the events calendar and sent by email as each event gets close.

🎥

Late October — Season Kickoff

Equipment distribution and an outdoor movie. Meet at the Smiths' house, get your kids fitted for skis, and start the season together.

📅 Late October 📍 Shaker Heights
⛳

November — Dryland & Ice Skating

A short dryland practice to build balance and technique off the snow, followed by ice skating at Thornton Park.

📅 November 📍 Thornton Park
🏁

December — Relay Race

A team relay at Chapin Forest — on skis if there is snow, running if there is not.

📅 December 📍 Chapin Forest
⛷

January — Technique Practice

Ski technique sessions with our volunteer coaches. We plan several dates in January and hold the ones where we have snow.

📅 January (snow permitting) 📍 Wherever we have snow
⛸

February — Technique Practice

More time on snow, plus a chance to put January's skills to work. We plan several dates and cancel the ones the weather takes away.

📅 February (snow permitting) 📍 Wherever we have snow
🥾

March / April — Season-End Hike

A family hike to close out the ski season. Please bring borrowed equipment back with you for return.

📅 March or April 📦 Equipment return
🏕

Late May / Early June — Camping Trip

An overnight family camping trip at Punderson State Park. Our chance to teach camp setup, fire safety, and Leave No Trace.

📅 Late May or early June 📍 Punderson State Park
🏃

August — The Guardian Mile

A group outing to run the Guardian Mile in Cleveland together. A fun, low-key way to keep the club connected over the summer.

📅 August 📍 Cleveland

How Winter Scheduling Works

❄️

Snow Decides

Northeast Ohio snow is unpredictable. We put extra January and February dates on the calendar knowing we will cancel some of them.

🕑

Sunday Afternoons

Most events are held on Sunday afternoons, which works best for the majority of our families.

📧

Email Confirmations

We confirm or cancel by email, generally at least two hours before the scheduled start. See our weather policy for details.

🌲

Off-Season Is Optional

Hikes, camping, and summer events are open to all members but never required. Come to what fits your family's schedule.
